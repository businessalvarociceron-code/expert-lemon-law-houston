Lemon Law Practice in Houston, TX

We work from 440 Louisiana St in Houston, Texas, and hear from vehicle owners across this part of the state, including Houston, West University Place, Southside Place, and Bellaire. A repair order, warranty booklet, or notice can start the conversation.

We look at repeated repair visits, warning lights, engine stalling, transmission shifting problems, brake concerns, electrical faults, battery failures, and air-conditioning defects shown in service records. We do not take on collision claims, routine maintenance disputes, or matters without a vehicle warranty issue.

During a first call, we review the purchase or lease agreement, warranty booklet, repair orders, invoices, correspondence, vehicle identification number, and dates the vehicle was unavailable. We then discuss the record and the next stage to consider; the call does not determine an outcome.
