Lemon Law Practice in Houston, TX

We work from 440 Louisiana St in Houston, TX, and hear from vehicle owners across Houston, West University Place, Southside Place, and Bellaire. Our intake team reviews calls and documents from these Texas areas, including warranty papers, repair orders, and correspondence.

We handle new car lemon law, lemon law buyback, warranty repair claims, RV lemon law, truck lemon law, and car dealer fraud. We look at troubles such as warning lights, engine stalling, transmission shifting problems, brake concerns, electrical faults, water leaks, and repair visits. We do not take on matters unrelated to vehicles or vehicle sales.

On a first call, we ask for the vehicle identification number, purchase or lease agreement, warranty booklet, repair orders, invoices, and messages with the dealer or manufacturer. We review the dates, mileage entries, stated concerns, and repair notes before discussing the next stage of the process.
